# Phase 47: Honey farming, hives, bees, and honeycomb

_Estimate: 1½ sessions._  
**Status:** done on `feature/honey-farming`. See [Bees and honey](../../features/farming/bees-and-honey.md). Built differently from this page: `what: both` was dropped (either click empties the hive), a placed campfire is left in place, `move_hive` was not built (the inventory port has no enchantments, so a silk-touch tool cannot be recognised; it is back in the backlog), and there is no breeding e2e (peaceful `NoAI` bees never pair).

**Depends on:** [6 Survival reflexes](survival-reflexes.md) (angry bees are a threat), [7 Gathering, crafting, smelting](gathering-crafting-smelting.md) (glass bottles, beehives, `Workstations`), [8 Hunting and food](hunting-food.md) (the animal catalog and its protections), [13 Construction and building](construction-building.md) (placing blocks, `SiteSafety`), [18 Farming, crops, animals, and trees](farming.md) (`FarmRegistry`, `build_farm` layouts, `tend_farm`, `inspect_farm`, breeding), [43 Campfire cooking](campfire-cooking.md) (a campfire as smoke: what a campfire block is and how it is read), [44 Farming extras](farming-extras.md) (`AnimalInteraction.use`, shears, the animal-product tools), [45 Composter and bone meal](composter-bone-meal.md) (`BlockUser`, bone meal for flowers), [Tasks](../../features/core/tasks.md). Optional: [9 Storage and containers](storage-containers.md) and [10 Item handover](item-handover.md) (storing and giving honey).  
**Needed by:** [48 Hardening and polish](hardening-polish.md).

Honey is the last big animal product the farming features do not cover (it was the first line of the backlog entry "Farming extras"). It has its own rules: a hive holds up to three bees, fills a level each time a bee comes back from flowers, is ready at level 5, and gives a honey bottle (with a glass bottle) or three honeycombs (with shears), and taking either **angers the bees inside unless there is smoke below**. So the phase is mostly about doing that safely, and about making more hives and more bees.

- Branch: `feature/honey-farming`. Three tools, `inspect_hives` (read-only), `harvest_honey`, and `move_hive`; a farm kind `apiary` for `build_farm`, `tend_farm`, `inspect_farm`, and `register_farm`; and `bee` in the animal tables so `breed_animals` and `lure_animals` work on bees. New ports: `HiveAccess` (a beehive or bee nest's block state: `honey_level` 0 to 5, nest or hive, bees inside when the server sends the block entity, `unknown` when it does not) and, from phase 45, `BlockUser` for clicking a hive with a bottle or shears.
- **Inspecting** (`inspect_hives`): every beehive and bee nest within a radius (default 32) of the bot or of a registered apiary: position, `nest` or `hive`, `honeyLevel`, `ready` (level 5), bees inside (when known), whether a **lit campfire stands below it** (within 5 blocks, straight down: that is what smokes the bees calm), whether it lies in a protected area, and the bees flying near it. Read-only, no walking except what the scan needs. Wild nests (forests, flower forests, plains with sunflowers, meadows) are reported as found in the world, like ores; they are not "someone's build" unless a protected area says so.
- **Harvesting** (`harvest_honey`): `what` (`honey` for bottles, `honeycomb` for shears, `both`: bottles first; each click resets the level to 0, so one click per hive per round), `at` (a hive), or `farm` (an apiary) or a radius, `count` (how many bottles or combs at most), `smoke` (`auto` by default).
  - **Smoke first, always.** The bot stands a hive harvest on a lit campfire **beneath the hive** (within 5 blocks below, in the open air between is fine): if one is already there it is used; otherwise, with `smoke: auto` and a campfire in the bag, it places one on the ground cell under the hive (never crafted on the way: the failure `no_smoke` says a campfire is 3 sticks, a coal or charcoal, and 3 logs, like the campfire phase), and picks it up again afterwards only if the bot placed it and the player did not ask to leave it (a placed campfire is otherwise kept, with its position in the result). A hive inside a protected area that is not the apiary's own is skipped. **Never without smoke:** harvesting an unsmoked hive angers every bee inside and near it, so the bot refuses (`no_smoke`) and asks; only the player's own words ("do it anyway") allow `smoke: never`, per call, and the tool says bees will attack.
  - **Tools.** A `glass_bottle` per honey (crafted from 3 glass when the bag has glass and the player allowed crafting; `no_bottles` otherwise) and `shears` for combs (`no_shears`). The honey bottle is checked in the inventory after each click and the level is read back (it must drop to 0); a click that did nothing is reported, not counted.
  - **Bees that get angry anyway** (smoke blew out, a hive was mined by someone else): the threat table learns `bee`: an angry bee (read from the entity metadata) is fought like other hostile mobs and a calm one is never attacked; the task stops and reports `bees_angry`, and the survival reflexes take over (run, fight, eat). Poison is treated like other damage over time by the existing reflexes.
  - Result: `honeyBottles`, `honeycomb`, `hives` worked, `skipped` (by reason: not ready, protected, unreachable, no smoke), `campfires` placed.
- **Bees** (`bee` in the animal tables). A bee breeds with any flower (`poppy`, `dandelion`, `blue_orchid`, `allium`, `azure_bluet`, tulips, `oxeye_daisy`, `cornflower`, `lily_of_the_valley`, `sunflower`, `lilac`, `rose_bush`, `peony`, and the rest of the game's flowers tag), as cows breed with wheat. `breed_animals species: bee` feeds pairs with flowers near an apiary; babies are counted like calves (they grow up in 20 minutes). Bees fly, so there is **no pen**: a herd target of an apiary is the number of bees seen within its flower area. `lure_animals` with flowers in hand leads bees toward a new hive (bees follow flowers). Bees are never hunted (`hunt_animals` keeps refusing them: they are not on the huntable list), never harmed on purpose, and a named or tamed one is left alone like any animal.
- **The apiary** (`build_farm kind: apiary`). `hives` (default 3), `flowers` (default: a patch of one flower kind around them, from the bag or planted with bone meal on grass if the player allowed it), `campfire` (default true: a campfire under each hive, so harvesting is always smoked, lit with flint and steel when one is in the bag; the campfire phase's lit check applies). Hives are `beehive` blocks from the bag or storage (a beehive is 6 planks and 3 honeycomb: the bot crafts one only when the bag has the honeycomb; it never takes honeycomb from a hive to do it unasked). They go on posts or fences at a height bees enter easily (2 to 3 blocks above the flowers, entrance facing open air). Empty hives **attract wild bees within about 20 blocks** by the game's own rules; `lure_animals` and `breed_animals` fill the rest. The apiary is registered as a farm of kind `apiary` with its hive positions and flower area, so `list_farms`, `inspect_farm` (ripe = hives at level 5, growing = filling, empty = no bees), and `tend_farm` (harvest every ripe hive with smoke, restock bottles, breed up to the herd target) work on it like on a field or pen.
- **Moving a nest** (`move_hive`): mining a bee nest **with Silk Touch** keeps the bees inside and the block; without it the nest drops nothing and the bees come out angry. So the tool needs a silk-touch pickaxe or axe in the bag (never enchants one, never takes one from a chest unasked), refuses otherwise (`no_silk_touch`), smokes the nest first as above, carries it, and places it where the player says (an apiary or coordinates); the bees that were in it stay with it. It asks before moving a wild nest that the player did not point at.
- **Not built** (stay in the backlog): honey blocks and honeycomb blocks as building materials, candles, waxing copper and signs with honeycomb (signs in this project are not waxed by the bot today), automatic bee-proofing of a base, bee farms with observers and redstone, and pollinating crops on purpose (bees do it by themselves).
- **Safety.** Never inside a protected area that is not the apiary's own; never breaks a hive or a nest (except `move_hive`'s silk-touch mining, and only on ask); never harvests without smoke unless told to; a hostile mob or an angry bee nearby ends the run as for hunting. Harvesting does not depend on the time of day (bees are inside at night and in rain, which makes it easier, but the honey level is what matters), so there is no schedule to keep.
- **Docs.** A new page for bees and honey (the animal farming, crop farming, and campfire parts of the gathering page get a line each), AGENTS.md (three tools, the `apiary` kind, and a protocol item: smoke first, warn before `smoke: never`, say how many bottles and combs), the backlog entry.
- **Tests:**
  - unit: the hive reader (`honey_level`, nest or hive, the campfire-below check, protected, unknown bees), `harvest_honey` over a fake world (smoked and unsmoked, a campfire placed and taken back, bottles and shears, the level read back, not ready, protected, no bottles, no shears, a click that did nothing, bees going angry and the task stopping, cancel), the animal tables with `bee` (breeding food, never huntable, angry or calm from the metadata), the threat policy for an angry bee, the apiary layout (hives, posts, flowers, campfires, registration), `tend_farm` and `inspect_farm` on an apiary, `move_hive` (silk touch or refusal, smoke, carrying, placing), the tools
  - integration: an apiary built and registered on a stateful fake world, hives set to level 5 through the fake, `tend_farm` collects honey and the levels drop to 0; `harvest_honey` without a campfire refuses and asks
  - e2e (`test/e2e/farming/`, extended): a beehive placed through RCON with `honey_level=5` and a campfire under it, bottles and shears given: `harvest_honey` returns honey bottles and honeycomb and the level reads 0 (checked through RCON); bees summoned with `NoAI` near a hive with no campfire: the tool refuses with `no_smoke` and no bee is angry; `breed_animals` with flowers near the apiary produces a baby bee (peaceful server, `NoAI` adults)
- **Exit criteria:** "get me some honey" with a beehive at level 5, a glass bottle in the bag, and a campfire under the hive (or one to place) returns honey bottles without making any bee angry; without smoke the bot refuses and says why; an apiary can be built, stocked with bees, and harvested by `tend_farm`.
