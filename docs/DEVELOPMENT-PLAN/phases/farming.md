# Phase 18: Farming, crops, animals, and trees

_Estimate: 3.25 sessions._

**Depends on:** [7 Gathering, crafting, smelting](gathering-crafting-smelting.md), [8 Hunting and food](hunting-food.md), [9 Storage and containers](storage-containers.md), [13 Construction engine and building](construction-building.md), [16 Site preparation: leveling, dug rooms, and opening doors](site-preparation.md) (optional).  
**Needed by:** [23 Leading a player](leading-a-player.md) (optional), [24 Autonomy, the bot's own will](autonomy.md).

Hunting takes animals from the world and never adds any, and food stops when the wild herds run out; gathering fells trees and never plants them. Farming closes those loops: grow crops, breed animals, plant forests, keep a steady supply.

**Design decision: layout is building, tending is not.** A vegetable garden or a pen *is* a buildable structure, so laying one out belongs to the construction engine (a new `PlanProvider`, executed by `PlanExecutor`, with `SiteSafety` and `LightManager`). But planting, harvesting, replanting, and breeding are recurring operations on *whatever farm exists*, including one the player built by hand, so they are ordinary skills with no dependency on the engine. Merging them into "building" would make every harvest a build plan; keeping them apart lets the tending skills work on the player's own farms and on ones the bot built.

- Branches: `feature/crop-farming` (layout provider and crop tending), then `feature/animal-farming` (pens, luring wild animals in, and breeding), then `feature/tree-farming` (groves and replanting). Start after the construction engine; the tending skills themselves do not need it and could go earlier if wanted. Two follow-ups after the three: `feature/sapling-collecting` (getting saplings by breaking leaves, on request) and `feature/discover-farm`.
- **Farm layout (engine clients):**
  - `CropFieldProvider`: parameters `width`, `length`, crop type. Tilled plots with a water channel so every plot is within 4 blocks of water, a fence with a gate, torches for light, optionally a chest and a composter. Needs a hoe and seeds; the plan preview lists what is missing. Tool: `build_farm` with `kind: field`.
  - `AnimalPenProvider`: fenced pen with a gate, sized for the species and herd (`entity cramming` limits the animals per block), water optional. `build_farm` with `kind: pen`.
  - `FarmRegistry` (persisted per world, like the container index): registered farms (built by the bot or pointed out by the player: position, size, crop or species). Farms are protected from `collect_block` by default; `list_farms`, `register_farm`.
- **Farm groups: "our farm" is several components** (a cow pen, a chicken pen, a pig pen, and several fields, each fenced apart). Each component is its own `FarmRegistry` entry, and entries can share a **group** (`register_farm` takes `group`, for example `home`; a group can be tied to a landmark or an area, so "the farm near home" resolves to it). Groups are a label over entries, not a new structure, so components can still be tended one by one, and a component can be built by the bot or by the player.
  - **`tend_farm` takes a group** and runs its components in a fixed order (crops first, then animals, with eggs collected for each pen), each under the same safety checks, with one time limit for the whole pass. A component that fails or is blocked is reported and skipped; the others carry on. The result is **one summary with a breakdown per component** (for example: wheat field 2 harvested and replanted, cow pen at 8 adults and 2 calves, chicken pen eggs 6 stored and 54 thrown, pig pen fence gap at x, y, z).
  - **`inspect_farm` is read-only** ("go check our farm"): for a group or one component it reports without touching anything: crops by ripe, growing, and empty plots; animals by species, adults, and babies against the herd target and capacity; eggs lying around; feed and seeds carried; and problems (a gap in a fence or an open gate, no water within 4 blocks of a plot, trampled farmland, light too low, a dead or missing animal count). "Check" means this tool first; `tend_farm` is for "do the work".
  - **`discover_farm`** scans around a position or landmark for fenced areas (`PenDetector`) and farmland, works out what each one is (species by the animals inside, crop by the plants), and **proposes** a grouped registration; nothing is registered until the agent (after the player agrees) calls `register_farm`. Player-built farms therefore need no description piece by piece. Nothing in it is ever broken or changed. As built (`feature/discover-farm`): fields (crop by what grows on the farmland) and pens (species by the animals inside; an empty fenced area with a gate is a low-confidence proposal) are proposed, each with the exact `register_farm` arguments, a free label, and the requested `group`; what a registered farm already covers is listed as `alreadyRegistered`; groves are not detected, and the `PenDetector` idea became fence blocks plus a flood fill (`findEnclosures`), since a pen is found from the fence rather than from an animal.
- **Crop tending (plain skills):**
  - `PlantCropsSkill`: hoe dirt and grass into farmland, plant seeds, carrots, potatoes, or beetroot on free farmland (wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot first). Never jumps or walks over farmland (trampling turns it to dirt): the standing spots are the paths and the field edge.
  - `HarvestCropsSkill`: only harvests fully grown crops (the block's age is at maximum), replants at once, and keeps enough seeds for the next round. Optional bone meal on young crops. Yield goes through the storage policy.
  - `TendFarmSkill`: one pass over a registered farm (harvest, replant, breed) with a time limit, so "keep the farm running" is a repeatable task.
- **Stocking a pen** (a plain skill, like tending: it walks up to whatever animals are actually out there and needs no engine involvement, only a pen that already exists — the bot's own or one the player built):
  - `LureAnimalsSkill` (`lure_animals`): given a species, a count, and where to put them (a registered farm component, or a position), finds nearby wild animals of that species with `AnimalScanner` (never tamed, named, saddled, or ridden — the same exclusions `hunt_animals` respects, since a lured animal that turns out to be someone's pet is exactly the kind of thing this project never does without being asked), equips the item that species follows (the same table `BreedAnimalsSkill` uses below), and walks it in: gets within the vanilla follow range (about 10 blocks) holding the item, then advances toward the pen's gate in short hops, so the animal keeps re-triggering its own follow behaviour, checking after each hop that it is still close enough to be following. The gate itself needs no special handling: walking through it is an ordinary `goto`, and the fence gate is already opened and closed behind the bot by the door-opening machinery from Phase 16.
  - **An animal that stops following is not chased.** If it falls behind, loses interest, or drops out of view after a few hops, that one is abandoned (recorded, not retried) and the next nearest candidate is tried, up to `count` led in or the search area exhausted. The result reports how many were led in against how many were asked for, and why each abandoned one was left behind (`lost_interest`, `unreachable`).
  - Stops with `no_animals_nearby` when nothing of the species is found within the search radius, and reports `pen_full` (a shortfall, not a failure, the same as a `give_items` shortfall) once the destination pen already holds the herd size `entity cramming` allows.
- **Animal breeding (plain skills):**
  - `BreedAnimalsSkill`: feed pairs of adults of a species the right food (wheat for cows, sheep, and mooshrooms; carrots, potatoes, or beetroot for pigs; seeds for chickens; carrots for rabbits), waits for the babies, respects the 5-minute breeding cooldown and the 20-minute growing time, and never breeds tamed, named, or ridden animals. It stops at the pen's capacity or the requested herd size.
  - `HerdManager`: keeps a herd at a target size by breeding up and by culling the surplus with `hunt_animals`, which keeps its breeding floor of two adults. Reuses `PenDetector` and the animal catalog.
- **Eggs** (in `feature/animal-farming`, after breeding; chickens lay an egg every 5 to 10 minutes, so a chicken pen produces them all the time and they lie on the ground until somebody picks them up):
  - `CollectEggsSkill` (`collect_eggs`): picks up the `egg` items lying in a registered pen or a given area (`DropCollector`, so it also skips what the bot threw away on purpose), never inside a protected area that is not the pen, and never by hitting or harming a chicken. Also run as a part of a `tend_farm` pass over a pen.
  - **Default split: 10% is stored, 90% is released for chicks.** Storing goes through the storage rules and policy like every other deposit (`ContainerSelector`; a rule for `egg` picks the chest, otherwise any chest with room); the stored share is rounded up, so with any eggs at all at least one is kept. The rest is **thrown into the pen** (`EggThrower`, a client of the `Gaze` and `WindowDropper` ideas: the bot stands at the gate or the fence, faces a free cell inside the pen, and uses the egg from the hand, one at a time with a short pause; a thrown egg hatches with a 1 in 8 chance, and on a hatch 1 time in 32 gives four chicks, so about one chick per eight eggs; thrown eggs land anywhere in the pen and never near a player).
  - **Per call or as a standing instruction the split can change:** `store_fraction` (0 to 1, default 0.1) and `release` (`hatch`, the default, or `store`, which keeps everything). `set_egg_policy` saves the standing answer per world (`EggPolicy`, mirrored as the standing instruction `egg_policy` so the briefing shows it), like `FoodPolicy`. An instruction from the player always wins over the default.
  - **One safety exception to "release the rest":** chicks count against the pen (`entity cramming` and the herd size), so the release stops when the pen already holds its target number of chickens (`max_chickens`, the herd size given to `breed_animals` or the pen's capacity); the eggs that were not thrown go into storage and the result says so (`released`, `stored`, `pen_full`). A player who wants more chickens than that says so.
  - Tools: `collect_eggs` (area or farm, `store_fraction`, `release`, and `max_chickens`), `set_egg_policy`; the numbers stored, thrown, and picked up are in the result.
- **Tree farming** ("plant a forest of 10 trees around our house"):
  - `TreeGroveProvider` (an engine client): turns "N trees" plus an area (around a position, a landmark, a protected area, or a registered structure; `around` a house means a ring at a distance) into sapling positions on dirt or grass. It keeps the spacing each species needs to grow (light and free space above, at least 2 blocks between oaks and birches, a 2×2 spot for spruce, jungle, and dark oak that only grow big from four saplings), keeps clear of doors, paths, and farms, and previews the positions before anything is placed. Tool: `plant_trees` (`species`, `count`, and an area).
  - Saplings come from the inventory; when short, `plant_trees` says how many are missing and does not go looking (see sapling collecting below). Bone meal speeds growth when the bot has some and the request says so.
  - `TendGroveSkill`: a pass over a registered grove: replants a sapling where a tree was felled (also after `collect_block` on logs, when the grove is registered), clears trees that grew wrongly, and reports growth. Groves are registered in the `FarmRegistry` like fields and pens, so a grove is not cut down by accident: felling from a registered grove is a deliberate `harvest_trees` call that replants.
  - Tools: `plant_trees`, `harvest_trees`, and the grove shows in `list_farms`.
- **Sapling collecting** (`feature/sapling-collecting`, a follow-up after tree farming; **optional and on request only**: nothing plants trees or gathers wood and goes looking for saplings by itself, the player asks for it in chat, for example "get me 10 oak saplings" or "chop 20 logs and bring some saplings too"):
  - Saplings drop by chance when **leaves are broken by hand or with a tool** (about 1 leaf in 20 for oak, birch, spruce, acacia, dark oak, and cherry; about 1 in 40 for jungle), so the goal is counted in saplings and the work is a budget of leaves. **Never with shears**: they make the leaf block itself drop and no sapling (`chooseTool` never picks them, and a test pins that). Leaves that decay after a tree is felled also drop saplings on the ground, so the skill first picks up saplings already lying nearby.
  - `CollectSaplingsSkill` (`collect_saplings`: `species` (any when left out), `count`, `max_leaves` (default 40 per sapling wanted, at most 1000), `max_distance`, `on_inventory_full`): a client of `CollectBlocksSkill` (so protected areas, lava, no tunnelling, and the full-inventory policy apply unchanged), which it calls in small batches of the species' own leaves (`oak_leaves` gives `oak_sapling`), counting the saplings gained after each batch. Stops when the count is reached, the leaf budget is spent (`leaf_budget`), or no more leaves are found or reachable (`no_leaves`, or the reason `collect_block` gave). The result gives saplings gained, leaves broken, and what else the inventory gained (sticks, apples).
  - **As a part of gathering wood:** `collect_block` takes an optional `saplings` count. After the logs are done it collects that many more saplings of the same species (from `oak_log` the oak saplings; from a group such as `logs`, any species), counting the ones the logs run already gained. Running out of leaves does not fail the logs: the result carries a `saplings` section (`gained`, `requested`, `leaves`, `stoppedBecause`).
  - Not planned: bone meal on leaves, or waiting around for leaves to decay.
- Tools: `build_farm`, `plant_crops`, `harvest_crops`, `lure_animals`, `breed_animals`, `collect_eggs`, `set_egg_policy`, `tend_farm`, `inspect_farm`, `discover_farm`, `plant_trees`, `harvest_trees`, `collect_saplings`, `list_farms`, `register_farm`.
- Safety and rules: farms are protected areas for other tasks; lighting and water are checked before planting; night work follows the threat policy; a tool that would trample farmland fails with a typed reason.
- **Tests:**
  - unit: `CropFieldProvider` (plot and water layout, every plot within 4 blocks of water, gate, materials), crop age reading, `HarvestCropsSkill` (mature only, replant, seed reserve), `PlantCropsSkill` (hoeing, free plots, no trampling), `LureAnimalsSkill` (right food per species, tamed/named/saddled/ridden excluded, an animal that stops following is abandoned and the next tried, stopping at the requested count, `no_animals_nearby`, `pen_full` as a shortfall not a failure), `BreedAnimalsSkill` (right food per species, cooldowns, floor, protected animals, capacity), `HerdManager` (breed up, cull down), `FarmRegistry`, `TreeGroveProvider` (ring around a structure, species spacing, 2×2 spots, avoided cells, sapling count and shortfall), `TendGroveSkill` (replant, wrongly placed trees), `CollectSaplingsSkill` (only the wanted species counted, the leaf budget, ground saplings first, never shears) and the `saplings` option of `collect_block`, `CollectEggsSkill` (only eggs in the pen, the 10% share rounded up and at least one, the `store_fraction` and `release` overrides, the standing policy, no throwing when the pen is full, no chest space falls back to asking), `EggThrower` (aims at a free cell inside the pen, one egg at a time, stops on abort), `EggPolicy`, farm groups in `FarmRegistry` (grouping, resolving "the farm near home", a component in one group at a time or several), `tend_farm` on a group (order, one failing component does not stop the rest, the shared time limit, one summary), `inspect_farm` (each kind of finding, changes nothing), `discover_farm` (fenced areas and fields found, species and crop inferred, proposals only)
  - integration: `build_farm` through the tool layer on a stateful fake world produces the expected block grid; plant, grow (fake time), harvest, and replant cycle; lure a set of wild animals from open ground into a registered pen, gate closed behind; breed to a target herd size; plant a small grove around a spot and check the saplings' positions, replant after felling
  - e2e: a small farm with a cow pen, a chicken pen, and a wheat field: `discover_farm` proposes all three; after registering them as one group, `inspect_farm` reports the ripe and growing counts and the animal counts without changing a block, and `tend_farm` on the group harvests and replants the field, collects the eggs, and returns one summary with three parts; a gap cut into a fence shows up as a problem in `inspect_farm`; eggs summoned as items in a fenced pen with chickens: `collect_eggs` picks them all up, stores exactly 10% (rounded up) in a chest and throws the rest (the chest count is exact; with 64 eggs at least one chick appears, the chance of none being under 0.03%); `store_fraction: 1` stores everything; a full pen throws nothing; `build_farm` lays out a small field on flat ground and the block layout checks out through RCON; seeds planted, crops advanced with `/data merge` or bone meal, harvested and replanted; wild cows summoned outside an empty pen are led in by `lure_animals` and the gate is closed behind them, then two of them fed wheat produce a calf; a pen is built and a herd bred up to its size; saplings planted around a point with the right spacing and grown with bone meal
- **Exit criteria:** "make me a wheat farm here" builds a watered, fenced, lit field and plants it; "harvest the farm" harvests only what is ripe and replants; "round up 4 wild cows into the pen" leads them in one at a time, skipping any that wander off, and reports the count against what was asked; "breed the cows up to 8" feeds pairs until the herd has 8 adults, without ever touching pets or the last breeding pair; "go check our farm near home" answers with one report over every pen and field of the group without changing anything, and "run the farm" tends all of them with one summary; "collect the eggs" stores a tenth in a chest and throws the rest into the pen for chicks (unless told otherwise or the pen is full); "plant a forest of 10 trees around the house" places 10 saplings with room to grow, and felling them later replants; "get me 10 oak saplings" breaks oak leaves until it has them or says why it stopped, and "chop 20 logs and bring some saplings too" does both and reports each.
