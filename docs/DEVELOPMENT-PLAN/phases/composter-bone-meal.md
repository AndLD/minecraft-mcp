# Phase 45: Composter and bone meal fertilization

_Estimate: ¾ session._  
**Status:** done on `feature/composter-bone-meal`. See [Fertilization and composting](../../features/farming/fertilization-and-composting.md). Two things were left out: saplings in a grove, and a registered place for a composter (`at` or the nearest one is used).

**Depends on:** [18 Farming, crops, animals, and trees](farming.md) (registered fields, `inspect_farm`, `crop-table.ts`, `tend_farm`), [44 Farming extras](farming-extras.md) (melons and pumpkins share the stem rule), [9 Storage and containers](storage-containers.md) (optional: bone meal from a chest), [7 Gathering, crafting, smelting](gathering-crafting-smelting.md) (crafting bone meal from bones).  
**Needed by:** [48 Hardening and polish](hardening-polish.md).

Bone meal makes a crop grow a stage at once. A player asks for it in one line: "fertilize the whole wheat field". This phase adds that, and a composter that makes bone meal out of scraps. It comes from the backlog entry "Farming extras" (composters).

- Branch: `feature/composter-bone-meal`. Two tools, `fertilize_crops` and `use_composter`, and a small `BoneMeal` port (or a method on `Placer`) for using bone meal on a block.
- **Fertilizing on ask** (`fertilize_crops`): `farm` (a registered field, grove, or group) or `area`, `until` (`ripe`, the default: apply until each crop is ripe, or `stages: n`), `max_bone_meal` (a cap so a big field does not use everything; default what is carried), and `crop` optional filter. It never runs on its own: the player asked for it ("on ask for the whole crop field"). For every unripe crop in the field (read with the same scan `inspect_farm` uses) the bot walks up, holds the bone meal, and uses it on the block; the game can fail a use (a wheat plant takes a random 2 to 5 stages, and a use on a fully grown crop does nothing), so it reads the crop's age after each use and stops at ripe. Order: nearest first, one pass, then a second pass for what is left. A stem crop (pumpkin, melon) only grows its stem: bone meal on a fully grown stem is skipped (the fruit is not affected), and the plot around it is never trampled. Saplings in a grove can be fertilized the same way (a tree may grow, and the tree rules still apply: never inside a protected area that is not the grove's own). The result: `applied`, `ripened`, `skipped` (with reasons: protected, unreachable, already ripe), `boneMealLeft`.
- **Where the bone meal comes from.** What is carried first; with `from_storage: true`, registered unprotected containers by the storage rules (the same fetch step as torches and fuel); with `craft: true`, bones in the bag or storage crafted into bone meal (1 bone makes 3). It never breaks skeleton bones or hunts for them, and it says exactly how much is missing (`missing_bone_meal`).
- **The composter** (`use_composter`): `composter` (a registered place, or the nearest within 32 blocks), `items` (what to put in: by default spare crops and scraps the bot carries and the storage rules would call surplus, never food it needs and never tools), and `collect: true` (take the bone meal out when it is ready). A composter has 8 levels; each compostable item has a chance to raise it (seeds, saplings, leaves, and crops are common; hay and cakes are sure): the bot puts items on it one at a time (using the item on the block) until it is full (level 7 becomes level 8 "ready" and a click takes the bone meal). It reads the level from the block state, so it knows when to stop and when it is ready. The result: `composted` (items), `boneMeal` (taken out), `level`. A composter is not built for the player (a composter block is 7 planks or logs; it is placed like any other block by `build`/`place_block`), and the tool never breaks one.
- **Safety.** Never fertilize or compost inside a protected area that is not the field's own; the field's own protected area is the usual case and is allowed, like harvesting; a hostile mob nearby ends the run as everywhere; nothing is thrown away.
- **Docs.** A page for fertilization and composting (the crop farming and tree farming pages get a line), AGENTS.md (two tools and a protocol item: ask before using a lot of bone meal on a big field, say how much was used), the backlog entry.
- **Tests:**
  - unit: fertilizing (unripe crops only, until ripe, a `stages` limit, the cap, a failed use retried, a stem crop, saplings, protected cells, cancel, the counts), the bone meal source (carried, storage, crafted from bones, missing), the composter (the level rules, items that compost and items that must not, full, ready, collect, none near), the tools
  - integration: a registered wheat field with half of it unripe, bone meal in the bag: `fertilize_crops` ripens it and `harvest_crops` takes it, through the tool layer on a stateful fake world
  - e2e (`test/e2e/farming/`, extended): a wheat field planted, bone meal given, `fertilize_crops` on the whole field: every plant reads ripe (checked through RCON); a composter placed through RCON and wheat seeds given: `use_composter` fills it and takes the bone meal out
- **Exit criteria:** "fertilize the whole wheat field" ripens every plant with the bone meal in the bag and reports how much it used, and the composter turns scraps into bone meal.
