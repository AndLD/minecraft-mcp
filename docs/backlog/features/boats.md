# Boats

**Status:** not planned.

The bot is on foot: it never crosses or sails on water. One capability, two uses that are left out today:

- **Crossing water in a search** (`explore_for_structure`, `explore_caves`): the search never leaves the shore, so islands and the far side of a lake or a sea are out of reach (see [World discovery](../../features/world/world-discovery.md)).
- **Fishing from open water,** and reaching a lake with no shore the bot can stand on (see [Fishing](../../features/gathering/fishing.md)).

It would need a boat or a raft placed and boarded (a `Vehicle` port for mounting, steering, and leaving, and the pathfinder treating a boat as a way over water), and never a boat left in a place that cannot be reached again.
