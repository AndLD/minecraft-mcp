# Farming extras

**Status:** not planned.

What the farming feature still leaves out.

- **Moving a bee nest with Silk Touch** (`move_hive`, from [Phase 47](../../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/honey-farming.md)): needs the inventory to know a tool's enchantments. Also picking a placed campfire up after harvesting, honey and honeycomb blocks, candles, waxing, and bee-proofing a base.
- **Sugar cane and bamboo:** they grow in place and are harvested by cutting the top, which is not a crop the field code knows.
- **Cocoa** (on jungle logs) and **nether wart** (soul sand, in the Nether).
- **Redstone auto-farms** (observers, pistons, and hoppers).
