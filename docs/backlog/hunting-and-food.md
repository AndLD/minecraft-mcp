# Hunting and food: gaps

**Status:** partly planned.

- **Campfire cooking:** planned, [Phase 43](../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/campfire-cooking.md).
- **Leashed animals** are not detected as protected (Mineflayer does not expose leads); the pen heuristic covers most cases.
- **The pen heuristic** only knows fences, gates, walls, and iron bars; a pen made of other blocks (stone walls, glass) is not recognized.
- **Foraging:** berries, mushrooms, sweet berries, and other wild food are not collected; only livestock is huntable and fish are caught (see [Fishing](fishing.md)).
