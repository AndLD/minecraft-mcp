# Bees and honey

**Depends on:** [Animal farming](animal-farming.md) (`AnimalInteraction`, breeding and luring, `FarmRegistry`, `build_farm` layouts, `tend_farm`, `inspect_farm`), [Animal products](animal-products.md) (shears, the animal-product pattern), [Fertilization and composting](fertilization-and-composting.md) (`BlockUser`), [Gathering, crafting, smelting](../gathering/gathering-crafting-smelting.md) (the campfire, glass bottles), [Survival reflexes](../survival/survival-reflexes.md) (the threat table).  
**Used by:** nothing yet.

`feature/honey-farming` ([Phase 47](../../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/honey-farming.md)). Bees, beehives, bee nests, honey, and honeycomb, with the one rule that matters: taking either from a hive angers the bees unless a lit campfire stands below it.

- **`inspect_hives`** (`InspectHivesSkill`, read-only). Finds every `beehive` and `bee_nest` within a radius of the bot or inside an apiary, and reports the honey level (`HiveAccess`, read from the `honey_level` block state, 0 to 5), `ready` (level 5), `smoked` (a lit campfire in the same column, up to 5 blocks below: that is the game's own rule, `hive-smoke.ts`), the protected area it lies in, and the bees flying within 8 blocks. The game does not tell how many bees are inside a hive, so that is not reported.
- **`harvest_honey`** (`HarvestHoneySkill`). `what: honey` uses a glass bottle (a honey bottle per ripe hive), `what: honeycomb` uses shears (3 honeycomb). Either click empties the hive, so one of them per hive per round (the plan's `both` option was dropped for that reason). For each ripe hive, nearest first: a protected hive that is not the apiary's own is skipped; the hive is **smoked first** (`smoke: auto`): a campfire already below it is used, otherwise one from the bag is placed on the free cell below the hive (over solid ground, within 5 blocks), never crafted; a hive with no smoke is left and, when nothing could be harvested for that reason, the task fails `no_smoke` saying what to do. `smoke: never` goes without (the bees get angry): only on the player's own words. After each click the product must have arrived and the hive's level must have dropped; a click that did nothing is counted in `skipped.noEffect`. After each hive it looks for an angry bee near it and stops with `bees_angry`, reporting what it had. A placed campfire is left where it is (`campfiresPlaced` lists it); picking it up again was not built. `craft_bottles` crafts glass bottles from glass in the bag.
- **Bees are a species now** (`animal-table.ts`): `bee` breeds and follows **any flower** (`foodsFor`; poppy is the usual one), so `breed_animals species: bee` and `lure_animals species: bee` work with whatever flower the bag holds (`holdBreedingFood` tries them in turn). Bees cannot be penned (`PENNABLE_SPECIES` leaves them out; `BREEDABLE_SPECIES` has them), and they stay off the huntable list.
- **Angry bees** are a threat. `AnimalInfo` and `EntityInfo` carry `angry` for a bee, read from its `remaining_anger_time` metadata (above zero while it is angry); the threat policy fights an angry bee within 6 blocks like any hostile mob and ignores a calm one, so the reflexes handle stings.
- **The apiary** (`ApiaryProvider`, `build_farm kind: apiary`, `hives` 1 to 8, `flower`, `campfire`). A row of hives three blocks apart along x, each **directly on its own campfire** (so the smoke is always in its column; `campfire: false` puts each on a fence post instead), and a row of flowers two blocks beside them (best effort, `passable` and `optional`). It is registered as a farm of kind `apiary` with species `bee`, inside a protected area like the other farms. Empty hives attract wild bees within about 20 blocks by the game's own rules. `register_farm kind: apiary` points out a hive area the player built.
- **In the farm tools.** `inspect_farm` on an apiary: ripe = hives at level 5, growing = levels 1 to 4, empty = level 0, adults and babies = the bees within 12 blocks of the area. `tend_farm` on an apiary harvests the ripe hives' honey with smoke (`honeyBottles` in the result; a missing bottle or smoke is reported as the problem); it does not breed bees (use `breed_animals`).
- **Not built.** Moving a bee nest with Silk Touch (`move_hive`): the inventory port does not carry enchantments, so the bot cannot tell a silk-touch tool apart; it stays in the backlog. Also: picking a placed campfire up again, honey blocks and honeycomb blocks, candles, waxing, bee-proofing a base, redstone farms, a breeding e2e (peaceful `NoAI` bees never pair), and counting the bees inside a hive.
- **Tests:** unit — the hive reader, `inspect_hives` (level, smoke, out or too far campfire, protection, bees near), `harvest_honey` (smoked, campfire placed, no smoke, `smoke: never`, honeycomb, not ripe and protected hives, `count`, no bottle or shears, crafted bottles, a click with no effect, stopping at an angry bee, one hive by position, cancel, an apiary with its own protected area), the apiary layout, the bee in the animal table, the threat rule, the angry flag in both scanners; integration — an apiary built and registered, `tend_farm` and `inspect_farm` on a ripe hive, the `no_smoke` refusal and `smoke: never`; e2e (`test/e2e/farming/apiary.e2e.test.ts`) — honey from a smoked hive with a bee beside it, a campfire placed under an unsmoked hive, the `no_smoke` refusal with the honey and the bee untouched, `tend_farm` on an apiary, and an apiary built on the real server.
