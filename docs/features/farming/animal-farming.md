# Animal farming

**Depends on:** [Crop farming](crop-farming.md), [Hunting and food](../gathering/hunting-and-food.md), [Construction and building](../world/construction-and-building.md), [Opening ordinary doors](../world/opening-ordinary-doors.md) (optional).  
**Used by:** [Egg collection](egg-collection.md), [Discovering farms](discovering-farms.md).

Phase 18's second sub-branch (`feature/animal-farming`; egg collection was in the plan's own text for this branch too, but was cut for scope into its own follow-up branch — see [Egg collection](egg-collection.md)). Covers pens, luring wild animals in, and breeding, matching the branch's own short name in the plan; `HerdManager` is the piece that ties breeding and culling into `tend_farm`.

- Branch: `feature/animal-farming`. New port `AnimalInteraction` (`src/core/animal-interaction.port.ts`: `hold(item)` equips it in hand, `feed(entityId)` right-clicks the entity with whatever is held), implemented by `MineflayerAnimalInteraction`. `BotLike` gained `activateEntity(entity)` (a thin wrap of Mineflayer's own method) since nothing in the codebase needed to right-click an *entity* before. A pure table, `src/skills/farming/animal-table.ts` (`BREED_FOOD`/`foodFor`), gives the one item each pennable species (`cow`, `mooshroom`, `sheep`, `pig`, `chicken`, `rabbit` — the same set `HUNTABLE_SPECIES` already lists) is attracted and bred by; it is deliberately its own small table, not bolted onto `animal-catalog.ts`, since hunting's catalog is about huntability and drops, not breeding food.
- **The gate is shut at the end** (`feature/closing-doors-behind`): `lure_animals`, `breed_animals`, `collect_eggs`, and `tend_farm` (pens) end with `PenGateGuard` closing an open gate of the pen, and say `gateLeftOpen` when they could not; see [Opening ordinary doors](../world/opening-ordinary-doors.md).
- **Gate side** (`feature/crop-field-options`): the pen takes the same `gate_side` option as a field (`north`, `south`, `east`, `west`; default south), since both use `fenceRingSteps`; the side is stored in the registry entry (see [Crop farming](crop-farming.md)).
- **`AnimalPenProvider`** (`kind: 'pen'` in `FarmLayoutRegistry`, registered alongside `CropFieldProvider`): a fenced pen with a gate for one species, `width`/`length` (3-12) and optional `water` (a single source in the middle) and `torches`. It reuses the exact same perimeter-and-gate algorithm `CropFieldProvider`'s fence used, pulled out into a shared `fenceRingSteps` (`src/skills/farming/fence-ring.ts`) once a second provider needed it. **The interior is cleared** (`fix/farming-fixes`): on uneven ground the interior kept grass and dirt at fence level, which animals cannot stand in, and the pen's own protected area then stopped anything from digging there. `generate()` now reads the world (`FarmLayoutContext.blockAt`, given by `BuildFarmSkill`) and adds `break` steps, before the fence and so before the protection is registered, for natural blocks and plants in the footprint at fence level and the cell above (`isClearable`, the site-leveling rule); air, grass, and water are left, and anything else found inside (a chest, planks, a log) is left, not broken, and named in the preview's `warnings`. Otherwise a pen is a handful of `place`/`light` steps around the edge — no `till` steps, unlike a field: `penCapacity(width, length)` is a simple one-animal-per-block figure, explicitly **not** vanilla's real entity-cramming limit (which is about several entities sharing one exact block, not a spread-out pen), documented as such wherever it is used.
- **A real bug found and fixed while wiring the pen kind in:** `BuildFarmSkill.run()` registered every component it built with a **hardcoded `kind: 'field'`**, left over from when `field` was the only kind that existed. A pen built through `build_farm` would have been silently mis-registered as a field, breaking `TendFarmSkill`/`InspectFarmSkill`'s kind dispatch. Fixed to register `provider.kind` (checked against the known `FarmEntry` kinds first, since the registry's `kind` is a plain string but `FarmRegistry.register` needs the literal union). `FarmRegistry` also gained `species`/`herdTarget` fields (a version-2 migration from the crop-farming branch's version-1 documents, tested directly against a hand-built v1 document) and a `speciesOf?(params)` hook on `FarmLayoutProvider`, mirroring `cropOf?`.
- **`LureAnimalsSkill`** (`lure_animals`) finds nearby wild animals of a species (never tamed, named, saddled, or ridden — `individualProtection`, reused from `animal-catalog.ts`; unlike hunting, a baby is not excluded, matching the plan's own exclusion list) and walks them, one at a time, into a pen or a position. The bot holds the food, **walks up to the animal** (re-reading where the animal is before every try, up to three tries, and a flock more than 30 blocks away in legs of 30 so one long walk cannot fail the trip), ends within 3 blocks of it, and then advances toward the destination in `HOP_DISTANCE`-sized (4) hops. **After each hop it waits** (half a second at a time, up to five seconds) until the animal is within 6 blocks again, since the bot outruns any animal and a hop made while it is far behind is how it loses interest; an animal that does not catch up, or that is more than 12 blocks away, is `lost_interest`, and one that vanishes from view is `unreachable`. Either way it is recorded, never chased, and the next nearest candidate is tried. The destination is either given directly or the centre of a registered pen's bounds; the gate itself needs no special handling, since `goToPosition` already opens and closes doors and gates it meets (Phase 16). **The reply** (`fix/farming-fixes`): `led`, `requested`, `room` (how many the pen could still take), `shortfall` (`requested - led`, whatever the reason), `abandoned` (each animal given up on and why), and `penFull`, which is true **only** when the pen already held its capacity before anything was tried (it was `led < count`, which was also true when nothing could be led at all, so a live run that led no chickens over a long trip reported a full, empty pen). Nothing of the species nearby at all is the one real failure, `no_animals_nearby`.
- **`BreedAnimalsSkill`** (`breed_animals`) feeds pairs of eligible adults (excluding tamed/named/saddled/ridden the same way) the species' breeding food and waits up to `BABY_WAIT_MS` (8s, polling every second) to see whether a new baby actually appears in the area, before moving to the next pair; stops once the herd (adults and babies together) reaches `targetCount` or there are no more eligible pairs. **Real Minecraft cooldowns and growth timers are not simulated or tracked** — the skill just feeds and watches for the result the server actually produces, which is honest about what it can and cannot know (it has no way to read a "still on cooldown" state directly), rather than modeling vanilla's internal timers from outside.
- **`HerdManager`** (not a tool of its own — used internally by `TendFarmSkill`'s pen branch, and could be reused by a future `tend_farm`-like caller) counts the herd of a species within a pen's bounds and either calls `BreedAnimalsSkill` (short of the target) or `HuntAnimalsSkill` (over it, culling the surplus with `keepBreeders: true, includePens: true` — the second flag matters: without it, `hunt_animals`' own `PenDetector` protection would refuse to touch an animal standing inside a pen at all) or does nothing (already at target). A pen with no `species`/`herdTarget` set is reported with a `problem` rather than guessing one.
- **A real, load-bearing bug found only by this feature's own e2e test, not by any fake:** every "is this animal inside these bounds" check across `lure-animals-skill.ts`, `breed-animals-skill.ts`, `herd-manager.ts`, and `inspect-farm-skill.ts` compared an entity's **raw, fractional position** (a cow standing at `x = 13.87`) directly against **integer block bounds**, instead of flooring first the way every other position-to-cell comparison in the codebase already does. A freshly bred calf near the edge of its bounds could read as "inside" on one check and "outside" a moment later as it wandered within the very same block, without ever actually leaving the pen — this showed up as `breed_animals` reporting `bred: 1` (a baby genuinely appeared) but the herd count moments later not including it. Fixed with one shared, flooring `insideBounds()` (`src/skills/farming/farm-area.types.ts`), replacing four separate ad hoc copies.
- **Not built, per the plan (cut for scope from this branch):** `discover_farm` (built later, see [Discovering farms](discovering-farms.md)). Egg collection (`collect_eggs`/`set_egg_policy`) was also cut from this branch, but shipped as its own follow-up — see [Egg collection](egg-collection.md).
- **Tests:** unit — `foodFor`/`PENNABLE_SPECIES` (animal-table.ts), `MineflayerAnimalInteraction` (hold, feed, `no_item`, `not_found`, `not_connected`, a thrown call wrapped as `failed`), `AnimalPenProvider` (fence/gate/water/torch generation, no interior steps, `defaultOrigin`, `speciesOf`, `penCapacity`), `FarmRegistry` (species/herdTarget registration and defaults, the version-1-to-2 migration), `BuildFarmSkill` (builds and registers a pen under its own kind and species, not hardcoded to field), `LureAnimalsSkill` (leads a candidate in, exclusions, abandon reasons, trying the next candidate, `no_animals_nearby`, `no_food`, `unknown_species`, `no_destination`, resolving a farm's centre, `penFull`), `BreedAnimalsSkill` (feeds a pair and counts a baby that appears while waiting, stops at target, stops with too few eligible adults, exclusions, ignores animals outside the area, `no_food`, `unknown_species`, `no_area`, resolving an area from a farm), `HerdManager` (breeds up, culls down with the right `hunt_animals` flags, does nothing at target, counts only the right species inside the bounds), `TendFarmSkill`/`InspectFarmSkill`'s pen branches (herd counts and actions, a pen with nothing set reported not called)
  - integration (`test/integration/animal-farming-flow.test.ts`, a stateful fake bot with real entities injected the same way `hunting-flow.test.ts` does): builds a pen and registers it under `kind: "pen"`; leads a nearby wild cow toward a registered pen; feeds a pair of eligible adults and never a named one; `tend_farm` on a pen runs `HerdManager` and `inspect_farm` reports the herd
  - e2e (`test/e2e/farming/animal-pen.e2e.test.ts`), against a real Paper server: builds a fenced pen and registers it; leads a real wild cow (summoned by RCON) into a registered pen (needing the same "wait until the bot's own view catches up" pattern as every other e2e suite, here via polling `get_nearby_entities` for the summoned mob); feeds a real pair of cows and gets a real calf back from the server, then `tend_farm` reports the herd against its target. **Gotcha found here, not simulable in a fake:** the breeding test originally used a tight 4x4 pen right around the two parents; a freshly bred calf wandering a block or two before the herd was recounted made the pen's own edge a real source of flakiness (on top of the `insideBounds` fix above) — the test now gives it a generous area instead of relying on a razor's-edge boundary.
- **Known issues (found in a live session, not yet fixed):**
  - **The gate was left open and chickens walked out (fixed in the closing-doors branch, see the entry above).** During a live test the bot went into a pen and left again without closing the gate behind it, and the chickens left the pen. The general rule and the probable causes are in [Opening ordinary doors, known issues](../world/opening-ordinary-doors.md); for pens it matters most, because an open gate loses the herd the pen exists to hold.
- **Exit criteria:** "round up 4 wild cows into the pen" leads them in one at a time, skipping any that wander off (reported, not chased), against what was asked; "breed the cows up to 8" feeds pairs until the herd has 8, without ever touching a pet or a named animal; "go check our farm near home" (`inspect_farm` on a group) reports every field and pen's state in one call without changing anything; "run the farm" (`tend_farm`) breeds a pen up or culls its surplus alongside tending any fields in the same group, one summary.
