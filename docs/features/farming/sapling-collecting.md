# Sapling collecting

Phase 18's follow-up after tree farming (`feature/sapling-collecting`). **Optional and on request only**: `plant_trees` never goes looking for saplings (it fails `no_saplings` or reports `saplingsMissing`, and says to use `collect_saplings`), no reflex or task collects them by itself, and `collect_block` only does it when asked for with `saplings`.

- **How saplings are had.** A leaf broken by hand or with a tool drops one by chance (about 1 in 20, jungle 1 in 40), so the goal is counted in saplings and the work is a budget of leaves. **Never with shears**: they make the leaf block drop itself, no sapling. `chooseTool` never picks shears (leaves read as `leaves;mineable/hoe`, so a hoe is used when carried, otherwise bare hands), and `tool-choice.test.ts` pins that. Leaves that decay after a tree is felled drop saplings on the ground too, so the skill first picks up saplings lying near the bot.
- **`CollectSaplingsSkill`** (`collect_saplings`: `species`, `count`, `max_leaves`, `max_distance`, `on_inventory_full`; `src/skills/farming/collect-saplings-skill.ts`) is a client of `CollectBlocksSkill`, not new digging code, so protected areas, lava, no tunnelling, the unreachable-in-a-row limit, and the full-inventory policy apply unchanged. It picks up saplings on the ground (`DropCollector`), then calls the collector in **batches of 8 leaves** of the species' own leaf block (`oak_leaves` for `oak_sapling`; every species' leaves the game knows when none is named), counting the saplings gained since it started (only the wanted species) after each batch. `max_leaves` defaults to **40 per sapling wanted, at most 1000**. It succeeds when the count is reached (it can overshoot by up to a batch, since every leaf of a batch is broken before the next look) and otherwise fails with a typed reason and the numbers in the message and `detail` (`saplings`, `requested`, `leaves`): `leaf_budget`, `no_leaves` (also `block_not_found` from the collector, and a batch that broke nothing), or whatever the collector said (`protected_area`, `inventory_full`, `unreachable`, ...); `aborted` when cancelled. The result also lists everything gained (sticks and apples come with leaves).
- **As a part of gathering wood:** `collect_block` takes an optional `saplings` count (1 to 64). When the blocks are done it works out the species from the block names (`speciesOfBlocks`: `oak_log` gives oak, a group such as `logs` or a mix gives any species), subtracts the saplings the log run already gained, and runs `CollectSaplingsSkill` for the rest. **Coming up short does not fail the logs**: the result gets a `saplings` section (`requested`, `gained`, `leaves`, `stoppedBecause`: `enough`, `leaf_budget`, `no_leaves`, ...). Only a cancelled sapling stage cancels the task. A failed log run skips the sapling stage. The resume arguments carry `saplings`. `CollectBlockTool` takes the sapling skill as an optional fourth constructor argument, so it stays a thin client and works without it.
- **Not built:** bone meal on leaves, waiting for leaves to decay, and getting saplings from a chest (that is `withdraw_items`).
- **Tests:** unit for the skill (batches and counting, only the wanted species counted, any species and every leaf kind, ground saplings first and no leaves needed when enough, the leaf budget and its default and cap, `no_leaves`, another collector reason passed on, success when the collector stops after the last drop, nothing breakable, no leaf kind known, cancelling, an unknown species, the inventory-full choice passed on), the two tools (`collect_saplings` translation and names; `collect_block`: nothing unless asked, the species being cut, saplings already gained counted, a group, coming up short, no details, cancelling, a failed log run, no sapling skill wired, resume arguments, range), `speciesOfBlocks` and the leaf/sapling helpers, and `chooseTool` never picking shears. Integration (`test/integration/sapling-collecting-flow.test.ts`): `collect_saplings` on a stateful fake bot where every leaf drops a sapling and `plant_trees` then plants them, `no_leaves` and `leaf_budget`, `collect_block` with and without `saplings`. E2E (`test/e2e/farming/sapling-collecting.e2e.test.ts`, real Paper): saplings summoned on the ground are picked up with no leaf broken; a block of persistent oak leaves is broken by hand until a sapling drops (`max_leaves: 100`, so the chance of none is under 1%: the one test in this feature that depends on the game's luck).
- **Exit criteria:** "get me 10 oak saplings" breaks oak leaves until it has them or says why it stopped; "chop 20 logs and bring some saplings too" (`collect_block` with `saplings`) does both and reports each; planting without saplings says how to get them and does not wander off.
