# Crop farming

**Depends on:** [Construction and building](../world/construction-and-building.md), [Persistent state](../core/persistent-state.md), [Gathering, crafting, smelting](../gathering/gathering-crafting-smelting.md), [Storage](../storage/storage.md) (optional).  
**Used by:** [Animal farming](animal-farming.md), [Tree farming](tree-farming.md), [Discovering farms](discovering-farms.md).

Phase 18's first sub-feature (`feature/crop-farming`; animal pens and tree groves are separate later branches). **Design decision, from the plan: layout is building, tending is not.** `build_farm` lays a field out as a `WorkPlan` through the shared `PlanExecutor`, the same engine `excavate`/`build`/`flatten_area` use; `plant_crops`/`harvest_crops`/`tend_farm`/`inspect_farm` are ordinary skills with no engine dependency, so they also work on a field the player built or registered by hand.

- Branch: `feature/crop-farming`. New ports `CropAccess` (`src/core/crop.port.ts`: `stateAt` — a crop's block name and blockstate `age`) and `Tiller` (`src/core/tiller.port.ts`: `till` — equips a hoe from the inventory and right-clicks the block), mirroring `DoorAccess`/`SwitchAccess`'s shape exactly. `MineflayerCropAccess`/`MineflayerTiller` implement them; `classifyCrop`/`cropFor` (`src/skills/farming/crop-table.ts`) are a pure table (`wheat`/`carrots`/`potatoes`/`beetroots`, each with its seed item and the block's max `age`).
- **A new `WorkStep` kind, `till`** (`src/skills/construction/work-plan.types.ts`), following the `seal` step's own precedent for extending the engine when a plan genuinely needs a new kind of action: satisfied once the cell reads back as `farmland`; `PlanExecutor` gained a `tiller?: Tiller` dependency and a `till()` method shaped like `place()` (equip-and-click, then poll for confirmation), reported through the ordinary `toPlace`/`missing_materials`/`incomplete` machinery — no preview-time material tracking, since a hoe is a tool, not something consumed.
- **`CropFieldProvider`** (`kind: 'field'` in the new `FarmLayoutRegistry`, mirroring `ExcavationRegistry`/`BlueprintRegistry`'s own shape): `width` (3-18) by `length` (3-32), a `water` layout (see below), every other plot a `till` step; an optional fence ring (one cell outside the footprint) with one gate in the middle of the side `gate_side` names (south by default) and, optionally, torches along it every 5 posts (a `light` step, `minLight: 8`). `defaultOrigin` mirrors `surfaceOrigin` (the ground block two east of the bot).
- **The field's `WorkPlan` does not plant it.** An early version placed the seed as a `place` step directly above each `till` step, in the same plan as the fence; building a plot larger than a couple of cells then failed with `stuck` ("no place to stand"), because `PlanExecutor` reserves the cell of *every* still-pending `place` step for the whole run, and a dense grid of reserved seed cells one block above every tilled plot (plus the fence ring) left `StandingSpotChooser` nowhere to put the bot for the plots in the middle of the field — found while building this feature's own e2e test, not in a fake. Planting was pulled out of the plan entirely: `BuildFarmSkill` calls `PlantCropsSkill` itself right after a successful build, so "build me a wheat farm" still plants it in one call, but through the same ordinary tending skill a later `plant_crops` call on the same field would use.
- **`FarmRegistry`** (`src/state/farm-registry.ts`), modeled directly on `ContainerIndex`: entries have a `kind` (`field`, `pen`, `grove`; a field was the only one built at first, pens and groves came in later branches), `crop`, `bounds` (two corners), a unique optional `label`, and a `group` (a new concept with no existing precedent to mirror, designed fresh: several components — a field, a pen, a grove — can share a group name such as `home`, and `byGroup()` resolves it; `tend_farm`/`inspect_farm` accept either a single farm reference or a group name, tending/reporting on every component in it). Registered in `worldState.components` like every other per-world state piece.
- **`BuildFarmSkill`** (`build_farm`) mirrors `ExcavateSkill` exactly: registry lookup, zod-validated params, `origin` resolution, `PlanExecutor.preview()`/`run()`, missing-materials fetched from storage first. On success it registers the component in the `FarmRegistry`, protects its footprint through the ordinary `ProtectedAreas.add()` (best-effort: a failure to protect never fails the build), and plants it via `PlantCropsSkill` over the plan's own bounds — plots still covered by the fence or the water channel are naturally skipped by `PlantCropsSkill`'s own screening (see below), so passing the wider bounds needs no special-casing.
- **`PlantCropsSkill`/`HarvestCropsSkill`** (`plant_crops`/`harvest_crops`) are plain skills over a farm reference or an explicit two-corner area, shaped like `CollectBlocksSkill`: no `PlanExecutor`, just `Navigator`/`Tiller`/`Placer`/`Digger`/`DropCollector` directly. `PlantCropsSkill` walks every plot of the area; a plot already holding something (checked one cell above the ground) is skipped, one that is dirt/grass is tilled first, and the crop's seed is placed. `HarvestCropsSkill` reads every plot's `CropAccess.stateAt`, breaks only a block whose `age` has reached the crop's own max, and by default replants at once, keeping one seed back in reserve (`SEED_RESERVE`) before spending the surplus. **Not built as a hard guarantee:** trampling avoidance is approximate — the bot approaches a plot to within a few blocks (`APPROACH_RANGE`) rather than a real path-around-the-edges planner, so a very packed field can still see it cross an already-tilled plot to reach one further in; documented in the skill's own doc comment rather than solved.
- **`TendFarmSkill`** (`tend_farm`) runs `HarvestCropsSkill` (with `replant: true`) then `PlantCropsSkill` (to fill any plot that was never planted) for a field, or `HerdManager` for a pen (see [Animal farming](animal-farming.md)), for a single farm or every component of a group, one summary per component; a component that is not yet a kind this handles (a grove) is reported with a `problem` and skipped, and a component whose own step fails is reported and does not stop the others. **`InspectFarmSkill`** (`inspect_farm`) is read-only: for a field it counts `ripe` (age at max), `growing` (below max), and `empty` (farmland with nothing planted) plots; for a pen it counts `adults` and `babies` of its species within its bounds; touches nothing either way. A grove reports zero counts (nothing to check yet).
- **`register_farm`/`list_farms`** mirror `register_container`/`list_containers`: a player-built (or otherwise not bot-built) component is remembered by its two corners, kind, and optional crop/species/herd target/label/group.
- **Gotcha found by this feature's own e2e test, not visible in any fake:** the real server reports a crop's `age` blockstate property as a **numeric string** (`"7"`), not a number — unlike the boolean `open`/`powered` properties doors and switches already read the same way. `MineflayerCropAccess.stateAt` parses either a number or a numeric string; `FakeBot`'s own crop-age property was corrected to match (a string), closing the gap so a unit or integration test exercises the same parsing path a real run does.
- **Not built, per the plan (left for later sub-branches or cut for scope):** egg collection, tree groves, `discover_farm` (proposing a registration by scanning, rather than the player giving corners; all three shipped in later branches), and a facing-aware `direction`/`for_player` for `build_farm` (a field or pen's own `direction` is not yet resolved from where the player looks, unlike `flatten_area`'s rectangle; both are placed with an explicit `origin` or default to two east of the bot, always oriented the same way).
- **Tests:** unit — `classifyCrop`/`cropFor` (crop-table.ts, every crop, seed-name lookup), `MineflayerCropAccess`/`MineflayerTiller` (state read, numeric-string age parsing, till-and-confirm, `no_hoe`, `not_connected`, a thrown activation wrapped as `failed`), `PlanExecutor`'s `till` handling (tills and confirms, already-farmland skipped, `missing_materials` with no tiller wired or no hoe, retries then `incomplete`), `CropFieldProvider` (till/water/fence/gate/torch counts and positions, no seed steps, `defaultOrigin`, `cropOf`), `FarmRegistry` (register, label uniqueness, groups, persistence), `BuildFarmSkill` (builds, registers, protects, plants, `missing_materials`, unknown kind, preview changes nothing), `PlantCropsSkill`/`HarvestCropsSkill` (till-then-plant, skip an occupied or untillable plot, `max_plants`, `no_hoe`/`no_seeds`, replant with seed reserve, resolve area from a farm), `TendFarmSkill`/`InspectFarmSkill` (single farm and group, non-field component reported not failed, a component's own failure does not stop the rest), the seven tools' input translation and task-launcher wiring
  - integration: `build_farm` through the tool layer builds and registers a small field on a stateful fake bot (extended with `setCropAge`/a numeric-string age property and a seed-to-crop block mapping in both `FakeBot` and `ConstructionWorld`, mirroring the water-bucket-to-water mapping `isPlanned` already had); `harvest_crops`/`inspect_farm` before and after a harvest; `tend_farm` harvesting, replanting, and planting an empty plot in one pass
  - e2e (`test/e2e/farming/crop-field.e2e.test.ts`), against a real Paper server: `build_farm` lays out a tilled, watered, fenced, planted field and the block layout checks out through RCON; `harvest_crops` on a hand-set field with one ripe (`wheat[age=7]`) and one growing (`wheat[age=2]`) plot harvests only the ripe one and replants it fresh, and `inspect_farm` reports the change; `tend_farm` harvests, replants, and plants a still-empty registered plot in one pass. **RCON changes need a settle wait before a tool call reads them** (the established pattern from other e2e suites): a `find_blocks` poll for the exact crop block at its exact position, before calling `inspect_farm`/`harvest_crops`/`tend_farm`.
- **Water layout** (`fix/farming-fixes`). One water block hydrates farmland only within four blocks in each direction (a 9x9 square around it, at the farmland's level or one above). The first version put one source every 8 rows down the centre column **starting at the first row**, so half of its reach lay outside the field: a 7x7 field hydrated only 5 of its 7 rows. Now `params.water` picks the layout:
  - `trench` (default): a line of water blocks down the middle of the field over its **whole length**, one line per 9-wide band of the width (a 10-wide field is cut into two bands of 5, not 9 and 1, each with its own line in its middle), so every plot is within four blocks whatever the length. It costs one filled water bucket per block of it (a 7x7 trench is seven).
  - `spots`: one water block at the **centre** of each 9 by 9 part of the field (`waterCentres`: equal parts, the middle cell of each), far fewer buckets (a field up to 9x9 needs one; a 5x32 field needs four).
  - Neither ever puts a lone block at an edge (a unit test checks every width from 3 to 18 and a range of lengths), and both keep every plot within four blocks of water on both axes. The width cap went from 9 (chosen for the single centre channel) to **18**, two bands. `build_farm` has a `preview` flag that reports the steps, the water buckets needed, and what is missing without changing anything; the buckets are made by [`fill_bucket`](../world/buckets.md).
- **Water goes in before anything is tilled** (`fix/farming-fixes`, found by the 7x7 e2e test). Farmland with no water near it and no crop on it turns back into dirt on a random tick, about once a minute per block, and the plan runs the plots one by one: plots tilled early on a big field were dirt again before the water was placed (`is dirt` in the failure). The provider now lists every water step before the first `till`, and the executor takes the first step it can do.
- **Trampled plots are planted again** (`fix/farming-fixes`, found by the same e2e test). Watching the field build on a real server showed that the plot next to the water block (with the bot standing on it, one block higher than farmland should be) turns back into dirt while the bot crosses the field to plant, and the crop above it breaks with it; it happened in most runs, always beside the water. `PlantCropsSkill` therefore does up to three **sweeps** over the area: after one that planted something, it looks again for plots that are dirt or farmland with nothing above them and plants those (tilling first). A sweep that plants nothing ends it, so a healthy field costs one cheap extra look (no walking for a plot that already has a crop), and `skipped` counts only the first sweep. **The final check:** after the sweeps, `plant_crops` looks over the whole area once more and reports `repaired` (plots lost while it worked and planted again) and `stillBare` (plots that are still dirt, grass, or empty farmland: no hoe or seeds left, or unreachable). `tend_farm` passes both on per field and puts a `problem` on the component when plots are still bare, or when planting failed (`no_hoe`, `no_seeds`), which used to show only as `planted: 0`. A live session showed the cause: the bot jumping over the single central water block while working the field lands on a neighbouring plot and tramples it. The cause itself is not solved (a path-around-the-edges planner is still not built); `harvest_crops` on its own does not check, `tend_farm` and `plant_crops` do.
- **The gate** (`fix/farming-fixes`). `fenceRingSteps` gave the gate `alignAxis: 'x'` on the south side, so the bot stood in line along x and the gate lay across the fence, a quarter turn wrong. The axis now comes from the side the gate is in (`gateAlignAxis`: a north or south gate needs `'z'`, an east or west gate needs `'x'`, the rule the house door follows), and the ring takes a `gateSide` (default south; the option on `build_farm` comes with the gate-side phase). The ring is shared, so pens are fixed too. `FakeBot` now gives a placed fence gate a `facing` the way the game does (the direction the bot looks at the cell), so the integration tests read it back; the e2e tests read `oak_fence_gate[facing=north|south]` from the real server.
- **Gate side** (`feature/crop-field-options`). `build_farm` takes `gate_side` (`north`, `south`, `east`, `west`; default `south`, the old behaviour) for a field and, because the fence ring is shared, for a pen too. The gate stands in the middle of that side and is placed in line with the fence (the axis rule from the farming fixes). The side is kept in the `FarmRegistry` entry (`gateSide`, document version 4; older entries read as unknown, `null`) and shown by `list_farms` and `inspect_farm`, so a later tool knows where the entrance is. **Decision: only the build-time option ships.** Moving the gate of an existing field (take the gate out, close the gap with a post, place a gate on the new side) is a small plan of its own and is not built; a player who wants it can break the gate and post by hand. `discover_farm`/`register_farm` do not know the side of a hand-built fence, so it stays unknown there.
- **Melon fields** are the same as pumpkin fields: `melon` is a stem crop in the table (see [Animal products](animal-products.md)).
- **Pumpkin fields** (`feature/crop-field-options`). `crop: pumpkin` is a **stem crop**: the seed grows a stem that stays, and the ripe stem grows a `pumpkin` block on a free cell beside it, which is what is harvested. Melons would be one more table entry; only pumpkins were asked for, so melons stay in the backlog.
  - `crop-table.ts` now tells `plain` crops (wheat and the others: harvested in place, replanted) from `stem` crops (`stem: pumpkin_stem`, `fruit: pumpkin`). `classifyCrop` answers for the stem block too (and `attached_pumpkin_stem`, which a stem becomes once it holds a fruit and which has no `age`), `stemCropOfFruit` finds the crop of a fruit block, `cropNameOfBlock` names a crop for `discover_farm` (`pumpkin_stem` is proposed as `pumpkin`).
  - **Layout:** rows run across the field (along x). Rows 0, 2, 4... are tilled and hold stems; rows 1, 3, 5... are **left untouched** (grass or dirt with air above) for the pumpkins. Whatever the water layout, every stem has a free row next to it, and the water columns of the `trench` layout cross the free rows too. `isFreeRow` in `providers/crop-field.ts` is the one rule.
  - `PlantCropsSkill` plants only in the rows that hold farmland (worked out once at the start), so it never tills or plants a free row; a stem-row plot trampled back to dirt is tilled and planted again like any other crop, and the final check counts only those rows. Without `pumpkin_seeds` it stops with `no_seeds` and a message that seeds come from crafting a pumpkin (one gives four) and that the first pumpkin has to come from the player or a patch in the world (the plant step of `build_farm` is best effort, so a build without seeds still succeeds with `planted: 0`).
  - `HarvestCropsSkill` breaks a `pumpkin` block wherever it stands above the ground level of the field and picks up the drop; it never touches a stem, does no age check on the stem, and never replants. `TendFarmSkill` needed no change: its harvest finds the pumpkins, its plant step fills any empty stem plot.
  - `InspectFarmSkill` reports a pumpkin field as: `ripe` pumpkins waiting, `growing` stems (any stage), `empty` stem plots with no stem, and `blocked` free cells that hold something in the way (a fence, water, and torches are not counted).
  - **Not done:** melons; a stem's fruit growing onto a free row cell beside the fence is harvested like any other, but the fence ring is not checked for fruit stuck between stem and fence.
- **Tests (crop field options):** unit — `crop-table` (stem crops, attached stem, fruit lookup, crop name of a block), `CropFieldProvider` (gate on each side, `gateSideOf`, default side, pumpkin rows tilled and free), `AnimalPenProvider` (gate side param), `FarmRegistry` (gate side kept, version-3 migration), `PlantCropsSkill` (seeds only in stem rows, trampled stem plot tilled again, free row never bare, seed hint), `HarvestCropsSkill` (pumpkins broken, stems kept, no replant, unreachable, aborted), `InspectFarmSkill` (stems, pumpkins, blocked, gate side). Integration — a field on each of the four sides (gate cell, facing along the fence, side listed by `list_farms`), a pumpkin field build, and harvesting a grown pumpkin with the stems still there. e2e (`crop-field.e2e.test.ts`) — a field with the gate on the east side over a real server (gate in place and in line, the south middle a post), and a pumpkin field built, one stem set to age 7 with a pumpkin beside it over RCON, harvested by `harvest_crops`, every stem still there.
- **Gotchas found on the real server** (`fix/farming-fixes`): farmland only becomes moist on random ticks, about once a minute per block, so the e2e tests raise `randomTickSpeed` for the check and put it back; a **wooden hoe lasts 59 uses**, so a field of more than 59 plots needs a better hoe (the bot stops with `missing_materials`, "There is no hoe to till with").
- **Exit criteria:** "make me a wheat farm here" builds a watered, fenced, lit field and plants it in one call; "harvest the farm" harvests only what is ripe and replants, keeping a seed in reserve; "go check the wheat field" (`inspect_farm`) reports ripe/growing/empty without changing a block; "run the farm" (`tend_farm`) harvests, replants, and fills any empty plot in one pass with one summary.
