# Animal products: shearing and milking

**Depends on:** [Animal farming](animal-farming.md) (`AnimalInteraction`, pens, `PenGateGuard`, `FarmRegistry`), [Hunting and food](../gathering/hunting-and-food.md) (`individualProtection`), [Gathering, crafting, smelting](../gathering/gathering-crafting-smelting.md) (`DropCollector`).  
**Used by:** nothing yet.

`feature/farming-extras` ([Phase 44](../../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/farming-extras.md)). Two tools take what living animals give without killing them, and the crop table learned the melon.

- **`shear_sheep`** (`ShearSheepSkill`). Works the sheep of a registered pen or area (`farm` or `area`), or those within `max_distance` of the bot. For each adult sheep that is not sheared it holds the shears, walks up, uses them (`AnimalInteraction.use`, a right-click on the entity), and waits up to two seconds for the sheep to show as sheared in its metadata; then it sweeps up the wool with `DropCollector`. The result has `sheared`, `wool` (gained, by colour, counted from the inventory), and `skipped` by reason. `count` stops early, `colour` limits it to one wool colour. Fails `no_shears` (the message says they are 2 iron ingots; it never crafts them), `no_sheep`, or `unknown_farm`. A sheep whose click changed nothing is counted in `skipped.no_effect`.
- **`milk_cows`** (`MilkCowsSkill`). The same for cows and mooshrooms, with an empty bucket per cow: holds the bucket, walks up, uses it, and waits for a `milk_bucket` to appear in the inventory. **Each cow is milked once per call**, so one cow is never milked in a loop and the result can be short of `count` (`buckets`, `requested`, `cows`, `skipped`). Fails `no_bucket` (3 iron ingots; it never crafts one), `no_cows`, or `unknown_farm`. Mooshrooms give milk, not stew.
- **Who is left alone** (`productSkip`): babies, named, tamed, saddled, and ridden animals (the same rules as hunting, with the mooshroom counted as a cow), a sheep that is already sheared, and, for shearing, a colour that was not asked for. The herd floor does not apply: nothing is killed.
- **Reading the sheep.** `AnimalInfo` gained `sheared` and `colour`, read from the sheep's `wool` metadata byte (colour in the low four bits, sheared in bit 4); other animals have neither.
- **Pens.** Both tools finish with `PenGateGuard`, so a gate that was open or could not be closed is reported as `gateLeftOpen`, like breeding.
- **Melons.** `melon` is in `crop-table.ts` as a stem crop like `pumpkin` (`melon_seeds`, `melon_stem`, the `melon` block). `build_farm` with `crop: melon`, `plant_crops`, `harvest_crops`, `tend_farm`, and `inspect_farm` work on it through the table. `harvest_crops` breaks the melon block (3 to 7 slices) and never the stem; `plant_crops` says where melon seeds come from when there are none (a melon slice crafts into a seed; chests in jungle temples, mineshafts, and dungeons hold them).
- **Not built:** crafting shears or a bucket on the way, dyed-sheep handling beyond the colour filter, honey, sugar cane, and the other items left in [the backlog](../../backlog/features/farming-extras.md).
- **Tests:** unit — `productSkip`, both skills over a little world that reacts to the shears and the bucket (filters, count, colours, skipped reasons, no effect, pens by label and area, cancel), the melon in the crop table and `plant_crops`, the wool byte in the animal scanner; integration — a pen with three sheep and two cows through the tool layer, all still there; e2e (`test/e2e/farming/`) — two sheep and a cow summoned with `NoAI` in a pen: wool and a milk bucket come back and the animals are alive; a melon field built, a ripe stem and fruit set through RCON, the fruit harvested and the stem kept.
