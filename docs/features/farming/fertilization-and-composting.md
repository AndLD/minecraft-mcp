# Fertilization and composting

**Depends on:** [Crop farming](crop-farming.md) (`FarmRegistry`, `crop-table.ts`, `CropAccess`), [Animal products](animal-products.md) (melons and pumpkins share the stem rule), [Storage](../storage/storage.md) (optional: bone meal from a chest), [Gathering, crafting, smelting](../gathering/gathering-crafting-smelting.md) (crafting bone meal, `DropCollector`).  
**Used by:** nothing yet.

`feature/composter-bone-meal` ([Phase 45](../../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/composter-bone-meal.md)). Two tools, both only on the player's ask.

- **`fertilize_crops`** (`FertilizeCropsSkill`). Takes a registered field (or a group of them) or an `area`, optionally one `crop`, `stages` (grow each plant at most that many stages; ripe by default) and `max_bone_meal` (a cap; everything carried by default). It reads every crop with `CropAccess` (the same scan `inspect_farm` uses, one block above the farmland), leaves ripe ones alone (`skipped.alreadyRipe`), and skips a protected cell that is not the field's own (`skipped.protected`, the rule the groves use). Then, nearest first, it walks up, holds the bone meal, and uses it (`BlockUser.use`), reading the crop's age after each use: a use can be spent without a stage, so it goes on until the plant is ripe or at its stage target, up to 8 uses per plant per pass and two passes. A stem crop (pumpkin, melon) grows its stem only; an attached stem has no age and is never touched. The result: `applied` (bone meal used), `ripened`, `skipped`, `boneMealLeft`. It never breaks or replants anything.
- **Where bone meal comes from** (`BoneMealSupply`). The bag first. With `from_storage` it takes bone meal, and with `craft` also bones, out of registered containers through `withdraw_items` (so a protected chest is never opened; one scan for a chest that was never opened, like torches and fuel), and with `craft` it crafts bones into bone meal (1 bone makes 3). It never breaks skeleton bones or hunts for them. When the bag still has none, the task fails `missing_bone_meal` and says how many unripe crops there are and about how much is needed.
- **`use_composter`** (`UseComposterSkill`). `at` names the composter block, otherwise the nearest within 32 blocks; a protected composter is refused. It puts items on it one at a time (right-click with the item held) while its level (read from the block state through `ComposterAccess`) is below 7, counting an item as used when it leaves the bag, whether or not the level rose (each item only has a chance to). At level 7 it waits up to five seconds for the composter to turn ready (level 8); with `collect` (the default) it then clicks it empty-handed, the bone meal pops out, and `DropCollector` picks it up. The result: `composted` (by item), `boneMeal`, `level`, `composter`. **What goes in:** named `items` (anything in the compost table, food included, since the player named it; `not_compostable` otherwise), or by default only the scraps carried: seeds, saplings, leaves, grass, ferns, and kelp. Food, crops, and tools are never composted unprompted (`nothing_to_compost` when the bag holds none). It never breaks a composter and never builds one (7 planks or logs, placed like any block).
- **Adapters.** `BlockUser` (`MineflayerBlockUser`) faces a block and right-clicks it, holding an item first when one is given; `ComposterAccess` (`MineflayerComposterAccess`) reads the `level` property. Found by the e2e run of the campfire phase: `bot.lookAt` needs a `Vec3`, so the adapter converts.
- **Not built:** fertilizing saplings in a grove (the tool works on crop fields and groups of them), a registered place for a composter (use `at` or the nearest), and dispensers of bone meal.
- **Tests:** unit — fertilizing (unripe only, until ripe, `stages`, the cap, a use that does not grow, stem crop, crop filter, protected cells, missing bone meal, unreachable, cancel), the supply (carried, storage with a scan, crafted from bones, bones from storage, cancel), the composter (scraps by default, unlucky items, waiting for ready, collect or not, named and food items, nothing to compost, no composter, protected, cancel), the compost table, the two adapters; integration — a wheat field through the tool layer: fertilized until ripe, then harvested; `missing_bone_meal`; a composter beside the bot; e2e (`test/e2e/farming/`) — wheat at three ages fertilized until every plant reads `age=7`, and a composter filled with seeds that hands over a bone meal.
