# Tree farming

**Depends on:** [Crop farming](crop-farming.md), [Gathering, crafting, smelting](../gathering/gathering-crafting-smelting.md).  
**Used by:** [Sapling collecting](sapling-collecting.md).

Phase 18's last sub-branch (`feature/tree-farming`). Groves are planted, watched, and felled by plain skills, like crops and animals: there is **no engine client** here (no `TreeGroveProvider` as a `WorkPlan`), because a grove has no blocks to build. It is a set of sapling spots, and planting a sapling is one `Placer.placeAt`. That is a change from the plan's own text, which had `TreeGroveProvider` as an engine client, and for the same reason as crops: a plan reserves the cell of every pending `place` step, and a grove needs no fence, floor, or water to justify the machinery.

- Branch: `feature/tree-farming`. No new ports and no new adapters: planting is `Placer`, felling is `Digger`, drops are `DropCollector`, what stands where is `BlockScanner`.
- **`tree-table.ts`** is a pure table (like `crop-table.ts`): `oak`, `birch`, `spruce`, `jungle`, `acacia`, `dark_oak`, `cherry`, each with its sapling and log, a `spacing` (least Chebyshev distance between two spots, about 4), and a `clearance` (free cells the game wants above the sapling). `spruce` and `jungle` can also grow the **big 2x2 variant** from four saplings (`canBig`, wider spacing and clearance); `dark_oak` **only** grows that way (`forceBig`). Mangrove (a propagule) and azalea are not covered.
- **`GroveSpotFinder`** (`grove-spots.ts`) reads the world and returns where saplings can go, placing nothing. For every cell of a rectangle it finds the ground by looking down from a few blocks above the rectangle's level (small plants and leaves are passed through; the first other block must be dirt-type: `dirt`, `grass_block`, `podzol`, `coarse_dirt`, `rooted_dirt`, `mud`, `moss_block`), needs the species' clearance above it (air, small plants, and leaves high up), and turns a cell down for these named reasons, reported in `rejected` with counts: `not_plantable_ground`, `blocked_above`, `uneven_ground` (a 2x2 needs all four ground blocks level), `unloaded`, `protected` (any protected area except the grove's own), `near_other_farm` (another registered farm's footprint, plus one block), `near_path_or_door` (a door, fence gate, trapdoor, `dirt_path`, or `farmland` within 2), `keep_out` (a ring's inside). Saplings and trunks already standing (a log only counts when it stands on dirt-type ground, so a house's logs do not) keep their distance too. It keeps every cell that passes, one spacing apart, and when fewer trees are wanted than fit, **spreads** them evenly through the list rather than crowding one corner. A rectangle over 3000 cells is refused.
- **`PlantTreesSkill`** (`plant_trees`): the area is a rectangle (`area`, `y` being the ground level), a registered `farm`, or a ring `around` a house or point (`from`, optional `to`, `distance` from it, `depth` of the ring: the keep-out is the box grown by `distance`, the rectangle that box grown by `distance + depth`). `preview: true` reports the spots and what the area could hold (`capacity`) and changes nothing. Otherwise it walks to each spot and places the sapling (four in a 2x2 for the big variants, all four or the spot is skipped), and **remembers the result**: a new grove in `FarmRegistry` (`kind: grove`, `crop` = the tree species, `spots`, `builtByBot`), protected from other tasks (height from below the ground to `MAX_TREE_HEIGHT` above) **only for a plain rectangle**: a ring's bounding box would cover the very house it goes around, so a ring is registered but not protected. Cancelling keeps and registers what was planted. **It never breaks leaves for saplings**: with none carried it fails `no_saplings` (the message says to `collect_block` leaves or fetch from a chest), and with too few it plants what it can and reports `saplingsMissing`.
- **Pointed at a grove with recorded spots and no `count`, `area`, or `around`, it refills** (`mode: replant`): the spots where nothing stands (`SiteState` `empty`, from `grove-scan.ts`) get a sapling again. This is what `tend_farm` calls, so the plan's separate `TendGroveSkill` was folded into it. Tending **never fells**: cutting a grove is only ever the deliberate `harvest_trees`. A grove without spots (registered by hand) has nothing to refill, so planting into it needs a count and adds its spots to the entry (`FarmRegistry.addSpots`).
- **`FarmEntry.spots`** (`{x, y, z, size}`, `y` the sapling cell, `size` 1 or 2) is a version-3 migration of the farm document (null for everything that existed before, tested against a hand-built v2 document); `crop` doubles as the tree species for a grove. `farmView` (`list_farms`) shows `spotCount`.
- **`inspect_farm`** on a grove reports `ripe` (a trunk stands there), `growing` (a sapling), `empty` (nothing) from the spots (`scanGrove`); for a grove with no spots it finds the trunks and saplings standing inside the bounds through `findBlocks` instead (so `empty` is unknown and stays 0).
- **`HarvestTreesSkill`** (`harvest_trees`): for a grove with spots, every spot with a trunk; for a grove without (or an `area`), every log standing on dirt-type ground inside the bounds (**not** a ring's bounding box, which is why a ring grove only ever uses its spots). It follows the tree by flood fill (26 neighbours, the same wood only, at most 6 blocks sideways, 24 up, 96 logs), digs the logs **lowest first** (from close by: within 3, then within 1.5 when the dig says out of reach), picks up each log's drop right after breaking it (**found by an intermittent e2e failure, one log in a tree not gathered, when the whole tree's drops were swept once at the end**) and once more around the base at the end, and, when the whole tree came down, plants a sapling on **every trunk cell that stood on the ground** (a 2x2 tree gets its four). Logs in a protected area that is not the grove's own are left. It works from the ground, and **for a tall tree it climbs** (`reach_tall: 'pillar'`, the default): when a log is out of reach even from close by, the `Scaffolder` plans a tower of dirt on a free ground cell beside the trunk (never in a protected area or beside lava, at most 12 blocks), pillars up, digs every remaining log within reach and raises the tower a block whenever the next one is still too far, and **always takes the tower down again** (also when cancelled or failed), picking the dirt up. It needs dirt in the inventory: with too little, that tree keeps its top and `scaffoldMissing` says how much was lacking. A tree that cannot be finished (no safe spot for a tower, no dirt, over the height limit, logs in a protected area) keeps the rest as `left`, is not counted as felled and **not replanted** (the logs above would block it). `reach_tall: 'skip'` keeps the old behavior. Leaves are otherwise not cut, they decay by themselves and drop saplings and apples. **A canopy in the way is cut through:** the planner (`Scaffolder.plan`, given `passable` and `maxClear`) accepts a column beside the trunk whose cells hold **leaves of the tree's own kind** (`oak_log` means `oak_leaves`, and not inside somebody else's protected area) and needs at most 12 of them cut, prefers a column with fewer leaves (each costs about as much as three blocks of walking), and does not mistake a leaf for the ground when it looks for the ground under the crown. Before each block of the tower goes up, the leaves in the two cells above the bot's head are dug (also above the planned height when the tower has to grow); nothing else in the crown is touched, and the drops (saplings, sticks, apples) are picked up when the tower comes down. `leavesCut` in the result says how many. A tree with no column even so is left as `left`. The result reports `trees`, `logs`, `left`, `replanted`, `saplingsMissing`, `scaffoldMissing`, and what was `gained`.
- **Not built:** bone meal (it would need a new port, like `Tiller`), collecting saplings by itself while planting (it is a separate, requested step: see [Sapling collecting](sapling-collecting.md)), `TendGroveSkill` clearing trees that grew where they should not, saving a grove as a landmark, light checks (the `LightReader` reads block light, not the sunlight a sapling needs), felling from above, `discover_farm`.
- **Tests:** unit for the species table, `GroveSpotFinder` (spacing and capacity, spreading, every rejection reason, finding the surface off the given level, leaves overhead let through, existing trees, a log with no ground under it, 2x2 sites, unloaded cells, an area too large), `PlantTreesSkill` (plant and register and protect, preview, a ring, too few saplings, none, a 2x2 needing four, an unreachable spot, cancelling, adding to a grove, refilling: only empties, none empty, preview, missing saplings, cancelling; every refusal), `HarvestTreesSkill` (bottom-up felling and replanting, branches, a different wood beside it left alone, saplings and empty spots untouched, `max_trees`, stepping closer, logs out of reach and no replant, tall trees (leaves: the planner's limits and what it may cut, protected leaves, `leavesCut`; climbing, raising the tower while a log is still too far, failing to raise, missing dirt, no safe spot, a failed or cancelled tower coming down, protected high logs, `skip`), protected areas and the grove's own, not replanting, a 2x2 tree, area mode ignoring what is outside, unsupported or on stone, a hand-registered grove, cancelling), `FarmRegistry` (spots, `addSpots`, the v2 migration), `InspectFarmSkill` and `TendFarmSkill` grove branches, the two tools (on a shared `test/fakes/grove-fakes.ts` block world). Integration (`test/integration/tree-farming-flow.test.ts`): preview, plant, the protected grove, `inspect_farm`, `no_saplings`, felling and replanting, `tend_farm` refilling, a ring. E2E (`test/e2e/farming/tree-grove.e2e.test.ts`, real Paper): preview then plant three oaks and check the saplings by RCON; a trunk made by RCON is felled bare-handed and its spot replanted; `tend_farm` refills a spot and leaves a grown tree standing. E2E (`test/e2e/tall-trees/`): a 12-block trunk is felled to the last log from a tower, the dirt is picked up again, the bot is back on the ground and the spot replanted; `reach_tall: skip` and a bot with no dirt leave the top; a full crown of leaves out to 4 blocks from the trunk is felled with only the tower column cut.
- **Exit criteria:** "plant a forest of 10 trees around the house" places 10 saplings with room to grow, clear of the house and paths, and `inspect_farm` counts them; "cut the grove" fells what is grown and replants each spot; "run the farm" refills any gone tree without cutting anything.
