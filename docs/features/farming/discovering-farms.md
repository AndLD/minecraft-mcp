# Discovering farms

Phase 18's last piece (`feature/discover-farm`): **read-only** scanning for farms the player built by hand, so nobody has to describe them corner by corner. It **proposes** and never registers: the agent shows the player what was found, and calls `register_farm` with a proposal's `register` arguments only after they agree.

- **`DiscoverFarmSkill`** (`discover_farm`: `around` a position (the bot when left out), `radius` 4 to 48 (default 24), optional `group`; `src/skills/farming/discover-farm-skill.ts`). No new ports: blocks, crops (`CropAccess`), and animals (`AnimalScanner`) are all it reads. `BlockScanner.findBlocks` searches around the bot, so for a place far from it the search reaches `distance(bot, place) + radius` and the results are then cut to the radius (the same limit `CollectEggsSkill` lives with). Only loaded terrain and animals in view are seen.
- **Fields.** Farmland cells are grouped per level (`clusterCells`, pure, in `discover-shapes.ts`) with a gap of 2, so a one-block water channel down the middle does not split a field in two. Fewer than 4 plots is a stray tilled block, not a field. The crop is the most common crop standing on the plots (`classifyCrop`; mixed crops are noted); with none the proposal is `low` confidence with no crop. The bounds are **tight around the farmland and the water between it, not a fence around it** (a fence ring in the bounds would make `plant_crops` till and plant the grass just outside it, since a ring cell's ground is tillable).
- **Pens.** Fences, fence gates, walls, and iron bars (`isPenBarrier`, now exported from `PenDetector`, whose own check is unchanged) are grouped per level and `findEnclosures` (pure) floods the free space from outside their bounding box: whatever the flood cannot reach is enclosed, as separate regions (**two pens sharing a fence are two pens**; a ring with a gap, a straight fence, and a diagonal-only touch of posts that lets nothing through are all handled by that one rule). A region is a pen candidate when it is at least 2 by 2, at most 62 on a side, and **at least 80% of its bounding box** (an L-shaped fenced yard is not a pen), and it holds **no farmland** (a fenced field is a field, not a pen). Species is the most common pennable animal inside (dead ones and other species such as wolves are ignored; mixed animals are noted). The proposal is `high` confidence with animals inside, `low` for an empty one **with a gate** (an empty fenced area without a gate is dropped). **The bounds include the fence**, like a pen built by `build_farm`, and `herd_target` is the herd right now, so `tend_farm` keeps it there instead of culling or breeding until the player changes it.
- **What comes back.** `proposals` (nearest to the place first, at most 20, `truncated` says there were more), each with `kind`, `confidence`, `bounds`, the evidence (`plots` and `crops`, or `animals` and `hasGate`), `notes`, and `register`: the exact `register_farm` arguments, with a **label nobody uses** (`wheat field`, then `wheat field 2`, checked against registered farms and earlier proposals) and the `group` that was asked for. **Anything a registered farm already covers** (its x/z footprint overlaps a found one) is left out and listed in `alreadyRegistered`, so discovering twice never proposes the same farm.
- **Not covered:** groves (a forest is not a farm until somebody says so; `plant_trees` is how groves get registered), fences on a slope or with a gap, and farms whose parts are further than the radius from the place asked about.
- **Tests:** unit for the geometry (`clusterCells`: touching, the one-block bridge, chaining; `findEnclosures`: a closed ring, a shared fence, a gap, a straight fence, a diagonal touch, an inner ring), the skill (a field with its crop, the bridged channel, bare and mixed crops, stray tilled blocks, separate levels, a pen with animals and its bounds, mixed and ignored animals, empty pens with and without a gate, a fenced field, two pens sharing a fence, a gap or a straight or huge fence, a ragged enclosure, walls, no fence blocks known, what a registered farm covers, labels and groups, nearest first, a place far from the bot and the radius, the cap of 20, an empty world, not connected), and the tool. Integration (`test/integration/discover-farm-flow.test.ts`): a hand-built field and cow pen are proposed, registering the proposals makes them known (a second discovery finds nothing new and `inspect_farm` reports both), an empty world, not connected. E2E (`test/e2e/farming/discover-farm.e2e.test.ts`, real Paper): a wheat field with a water channel and a fenced pen with three cows built by RCON are proposed with the right crop and species (asking again until the bot's own view matches, the established RCON pattern), nothing is changed or registered by the scan, the proposals register, and `inspect_farm` on the group counts the ripe plots and the cows; an empty arena finds nothing.
- **Exit criteria:** "go look at what I built" (`discover_farm`) reports each field and pen found, with the crop or species and how sure it is, and registers nothing; after the player agrees, `register_farm` with a proposal's arguments makes `inspect_farm` and `tend_farm` work on it, and discovering again proposes nothing that is already registered.
