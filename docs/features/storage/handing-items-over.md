# Handing items over

**Depends on:** [Storage](storage.md), [Drop recovery](drop-recovery.md) (optional).  
**Used by:** [Drop recovery](drop-recovery.md) (optional).

- `give_items` (`GiveItemsSkill`): `RequestPlanner` + `ItemResolver` turn the player's words into concrete items and amounts (`count`, `stacks`, or `all`). The resolver knows exact item names, plurals, everyday words (`cobble`, `steak`), and the item groups of the storage feature; when several things fit ("iron", "wood") it prefers what the bot carries or has in chests, and if that still leaves more than one it fails with `ambiguous` and the candidates (with counts), so the agent asks. A group takes the item there is most of first. Requests **by group** leave out the reserve (tools, weapons, armor, food, torches, buckets) unless `keep_reserve` is false; an exactly named item is always given.
- Source order: the inventory, then containers (`WithdrawItemsSkill`, never protected ones); when the known containers do not have enough it looks into the others once (`ScanContainersSkill`) and retries. It gives what is available and reports the rest as `shortfall`; with nothing available at all it fails with `shortfall`.
- Delivery (`mode: drop`): find the player (`player_not_online`, `player_not_visible` when out of view, for example far away or in another dimension), walk to 1.5 blocks, check the spot (`DropSpot`: no lava, fire, or water, and solid ground), face the ground just ahead toward the player, throw each item (`ItemThrower`, 300 ms apart), step back 4 blocks so the bot does not pick its own items up, and wait up to 8 s to see whether they vanish; what still lies there is `unclaimed` and goes to drop recovery (see below), which takes it back after a minute unless told not to. `mode: chest` deposits into the named chest (or by the storage rules) and says which; `drop_items` throws at the bot's own feet (junk) and steps away.
- **Throwing is done by clicking** (`WindowDropper`: Ctrl+Q per whole stack, Q per single item for small parts, or pick up the stack and put the part to keep back), with syncs after every step, for the same reason as crafting and chests. The window is `bot.inventoryWindow`, installed after spawn.
- **Gotcha found by the e2e test:** a throw sent right after `bot.lookAt` flew in the *old* direction, past the player and out of their pickup reach, about half of the time. The server learns the new look with the bot's next movement packets, so `MineflayerGaze` waits four ticks after looking.

## Taking an item the player hands over (`receive_item`)

`feature/receive-item` ([Phase 46](../../DEVELOPMENT-PLAN/phases/receive-item.md)), the reverse of `give_items`. `ReceiveItemSkill` is built from the player lookup, `Navigator`, `Gaze`, `DroppedItemScanner`, `DropCollector`, the chat port, and `Combatant.equipBestGear`; it adds no port.

- **What it does.** Looks up the player (`from_player`, the owner by default: `player_offline` or `player_lost` when they are not there or out of view), notes every item lying within 24 blocks as a **baseline**, walks to 1.5 blocks from them when it is farther than 3, faces them, says once in chat "Ready: throw me the pickaxe", and then polls every half second (the injected scheduler). Only an item that **was not in the baseline**, matches `item`, and lies within 6 blocks of where the player stood counts; the `DropIgnoreList` is not consulted, so a thrown item is never mistaken for one the bot dropped. It is picked up with `DropCollector`, and the gain in the inventory is what is reported (`received`) and said in chat ("Got 1 diamond pickaxe"). Without `count` it ends two seconds after the first pick-up (for a second item of the same throw); with `count` when that many are in.
- **What `item` means.** An item name, a group (`tools`, `food`, `wool`, ...), or a word such as `pickaxe` or `sword` (any item that ends in it, plural or not); nothing means anything thrown.
- **`use_it`.** Calls `equipBestGear` afterwards and says what it put on; the result has `equipped`. It only decides among weapons and armor, so a pickaxe is carried, not held.
- **Ends.** `nothing_received` after `timeout_seconds` (default 30, up to 300; one line in chat, never waits forever), `inventory_full` (the item stays on the ground, the message says where, and the bot says so in chat), `player_offline`, `player_lost` (out of view for 10 seconds), `aborted`. Nothing is ever taken from a player's hands or a chest. The plan called the wait `wait_seconds`; that name already means how long a tool call blocks for a task, so it is `timeout_seconds`.
- **Gotchas.** The baseline is taken before the bot walks, so an item thrown while it is still walking does not count as new: the agent should wait for the "Ready" line (the task's progress reads "Waiting for ... to throw something") before the player throws. Walking past an old drop picks it up by the game's own rules; it is not counted, because the gain is measured after the walk.
- **Tests:** unit — the skill over a scripted world (walking up and facing, near player, baseline and wrong or far items left, group and count, `use_it`, `nothing_received`, offline and lost player, full inventory, cancel); integration — the tool layer with a scripted throw and an old drop left alone, and `nothing_received`; e2e (`test/e2e/item-handover/`) — an item summoned beside the owner after the bot started listening is taken and held, an older one is left.
